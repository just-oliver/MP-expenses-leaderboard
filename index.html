What Do These Expenses Cover?

👥

Staffing

Salaries for MPs' parliamentary and constituency staff. The largest expense category for virtually every MP (up to ~£220,000/year).

🏢

Office Costs

Rent for constituency offices, office equipment, IT, communications, stationary and other running costs (up to £27,000/year).

🏠

Accommodation

London accommodation costs for MPs whose constituencies are outside London. London-based MPs cannot claim this (up to ~£28,000/year).

🚂

Travel

Rail, air and road travel for parliamentary business between Westminster and the constituency. Higher for MPs from Scotland, Wales and the north.

Important context: These figures represent the running costs of MPs' offices and parliamentary duties — not personal income. The money pays for staff salaries, office rent, travel to Westminster and back, and accommodation during parliamentary sessions. MPs themselves receive a separate salary of ~£91,346 per year (2024–25).
